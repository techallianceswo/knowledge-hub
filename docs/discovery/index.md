# Discovery stage

## Understand your customers and explore the opportunity

You have an idea worth exploring. Now it's time to learn more about the people you're hoping to serve, the challenges they face, and how your proposed solution might fit into their lives or businesses.

The Discovery stage helps you move beyond assumptions by gathering insights, understanding customer needs, and exploring the market opportunity in greater detail.

You don't need to have a finished product or all the answers. This is your opportunity to listen, ask questions, and use what you learn to shape your next steps.

## What you'll explore

The resources in this section can help you:

- **Understand your customers:** Learn more about your potential customers, their needs, behaviours, and priorities.
- **Explore customer problems:** Investigate the challenges people experience and how they currently address them.
- **Conduct customer discovery:** Learn how to approach conversations, ask useful questions, and gather meaningful feedback.
- **Assess the market opportunity:** Explore market demand, existing alternatives, and potential opportunities for your idea.
- **Refine your assumptions:** Use what you learn to revisit your initial thinking and identify areas that need further investigation.

## How to use these resources

Explore the topics available in this section to build a deeper understanding of your potential customers and the opportunity you're considering.

Each topic may include practical notes, tools, templates, or links to additional reading that can help you plan your research, gather insights, and organize your findings.

If your TechAlliance advisor has recommended specific resources, start there. You can return to other topics as your questions and priorities evolve.

## Questions to consider

As you explore your opportunity, think about:

- Who are my potential customers, and what matters most to them?
- What challenges are they experiencing?
- How are they currently solving these problems?
- What have I learned from speaking with potential customers?
- What assumptions have been supported or challenged by my research?
- What do I still need to understand before testing my proposed solution?

Use these questions to guide your research, reflect on your findings, and prepare for conversations with your TechAlliance advisor.

## Your next step

Start with the topic that helps you better understand your customers or address an important question about your opportunity.

Use what you learn to refine your understanding of the problem, identify potential opportunities, and prepare to test your assumptions in the Validation stage.
