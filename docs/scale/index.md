# Scale stage

## Grow your business and explore new opportunities

You've developed your solution, built your business foundations, and started establishing how your company operates. Now it's time to explore what comes next.

The Scale stage helps you navigate the challenges and opportunities that come with growing a business, from reaching new customers and expanding into new markets to strengthening your team and managing increasing demand.

Growth brings new decisions, responsibilities, and challenges. This is your opportunity to build on what you've achieved, identify new opportunities, and prepare your business for its next stage.

## What you'll explore

The resources in this section can help you:

- **Develop your growth strategy:** Explore approaches to setting growth goals, identifying opportunities, and planning your next steps.
- **Expand your market reach:** Learn about reaching new customers, entering new markets, and developing strategies to increase sales.
- **Strengthen your team and leadership:** Explore hiring, leadership development, delegation, and organizational structures that support a growing business.
- **Access capital for growth:** Understand different funding and investment options and how they may support your expansion plans.
- **Scale your operations:** Explore ways to improve processes, systems, and infrastructure to manage increasing demand.
- **Build strategic partnerships:** Identify opportunities to collaborate with other businesses, industry experts, and partners.
- **Measure your performance:** Learn how to track key business metrics, evaluate progress, and make informed decisions as your company grows.

## How to use these resources

Explore the topics available in this section to better understand the opportunities and challenges involved in scaling your business.

Each topic may include practical notes, tools, templates, or links to additional reading that can help you evaluate growth opportunities, develop strategies, and strengthen your business.

If your TechAlliance advisor has recommended specific resources, start there. You can return to other topics as your business goals and priorities evolve.

## Questions to consider

As you plan for growth, think about:

- What are my most important growth goals, and how will I measure progress?
- What opportunities exist to reach new customers or enter new markets?
- Can my current operations, systems, and processes support increased demand?
- What skills, leadership capabilities, or additional team members will I need?
- What financial resources will support my growth plans?
- What partnerships or connections could help my business expand?
- What risks or challenges should I prepare for as my business grows?

Use these questions to evaluate your readiness for growth, identify areas that need attention, and prepare for conversations with your TechAlliance advisor.
