# Efficiency stage

## Build the foundations for a stronger business

You've explored your opportunity, investigated the problem, and started testing your solution. Now it's time to focus on the foundations that will help you turn that solution into a business that can operate effectively and grow.

The Efficiency stage helps you think through the practical aspects of running your business, from managing finances and organizing operations to developing processes and making the most of your resources.

You don't need to have everything figured out from the start. This is your opportunity to build the structure, habits, and systems that will support your business as it develops.

## What you'll explore

The resources in this section can help you:

- **Develop your business model:** Explore how your business will create, deliver, and capture value.
- **Plan your finances:** Understand costs, revenue, pricing, cash flow, and the financial decisions involved in running your business.
- **Build your operations:** Explore the processes, tools, and systems needed to deliver your solution and manage day-to-day activities.
- **Manage your resources:** Consider how to use your time, budget, technology, and available expertise effectively.
- **Establish your business foundations:** Learn about important considerations such as business structure, legal requirements, contracts, and other administrative responsibilities.
- **Prepare your team:** Explore roles, responsibilities, and approaches to building a team that supports your business goals.

## How to use these resources

Explore the topics available in this section to understand the different elements involved in building and operating your business.

Each topic may include practical notes, tools, templates, or links to additional reading that can help you plan your operations, organize your resources, and make informed business decisions.

If your TechAlliance advisor has recommended specific resources, start there. You can return to other topics as your business needs and priorities evolve.

## Questions to consider

As you build your business foundations, think about:

- How will my business generate revenue and manage its costs?
- What processes and systems do I need to deliver my solution consistently?
- What legal, financial, and administrative responsibilities should I understand?
- How can I make better use of my time, budget, and other resources?
- What skills, roles, or external expertise will my business need?
- What challenges could affect my ability to operate effectively as demand grows?

Use these questions to identify gaps in your business foundations, prioritize improvements, and prepare for conversations with your TechAlliance advisor.
